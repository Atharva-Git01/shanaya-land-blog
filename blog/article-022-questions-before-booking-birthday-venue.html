Questions to Ask Before Booking a Birthday Venue

Use this venue checklist to compare capacity, access, setup time, included furniture, rules, accessibility, and the full booking terms.

Published November 19, 2026 · Updated November 20, 2026

Short answer

A venue can look right in photos and still be difficult to use on the day. Ask questions that reveal how the room works for your actual guest count, food plan, decorations, access needs, and schedule. Get important answers in writing before paying a deposit.

Capacity and layout

  • What is the comfortable capacity for the layout I need, not only the maximum standing capacity?
  • Can I see a floor plan or visit the room before booking?
  • Where can guests sit, eat, dance, and move between activities?
  • Are accessible routes, seating, and restroom facilities available?

Access, setup, and what is included

  • What time can I enter to decorate, and when must everything be removed?
  • Which tables, chairs, linens, sound equipment, and serving items are included?
  • Are outside food, cake, decorations, or vendors allowed?
  • Are there restrictions on candles, balloons, confetti, music, or wall attachments?

Costs and terms

  • What is the full price, including deposits, taxes, staffing, cleaning, and overtime?
  • What are the payment schedule, cancellation terms, and rescheduling policy?
  • Who is the day-of contact, and what happens if the room is unavailable?
  • What insurance, permits, or safety requirements apply to my event?

Ask for answers in writing and keep the agreement with your planning notes. Rules depend on the venue and location.

Venue visit checklist

  1. Walk the route from entrance to party room and restrooms.
  2. Check the room with your expected tables, food station, and activity space in mind.
  3. Confirm delivery, setup, and pickup windows.
  4. Record the venue contact and emergency or after-hours process.

Quick checklist

  • Confirm usable capacity and layout
  • Ask about setup and removal windows
  • List included furniture and equipment
  • Check rules, access, and outside vendors
  • Review all charges and cancellation terms in writing

Frequently asked questions

Should I get venue answers in writing?

Yes. Keep written confirmation of included items, timing, restrictions, fees, and cancellation terms.

Can I assume the maximum capacity will be comfortable?

No. Ask about capacity for your specific seating, food, and activity layout.
