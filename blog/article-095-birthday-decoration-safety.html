How to Keep Birthday Decorations Safe and Stable

Keep birthday decor attractive while protecting exits, walkways, candles, electrical fixtures, and guests from unstable stands, cords, and balloons.

Short answer: Place decorations where they cannot fall into food, block a route, cause a trip, or interfere with fixtures. Check venue rules and product safety instructions.

Protect walkways and exits

Before decorating, identify entrances, exits, restroom routes, and main paths. Keep cords, ribbons, balloon strings, gifts, and signs out of these areas. Put backdrops away from doors and busy routes.

Walk the space after setup as a guest would. Check that chairs, cake tables, and stands do not wobble or narrow the route.

Use candles and lights carefully

Use stable holders on heat-safe surfaces, keep flames away from paper, fabric, balloons, and flowers, and never leave lit candles unattended. A flameless candle may be a better fit where open flames are unsuitable. Keep electrical cords out of walkways and follow venue rules.

Do not attach decor to sprinklers, smoke detectors, or fixtures. Ask the venue before using candles or hanging decorations.

Secure balloons and hanging pieces

Use suitable balloon weights and approved hanging points. Keep strings away from faces and doorways. Foil balloons can create hazards near power lines; follow local utility guidance and dispose of damaged balloons promptly.

Safety checklist

  • Confirm venue rules for flames, hanging items, and electrical decor.
  • Keep exits and routes clear.
  • Stabilize signs, stands, and tables before guests arrive.
  • Keep flames supervised and away from combustible materials.
  • Secure balloon weights and manage cords and ribbons.

Frequently asked questions

Can decorations go near candles?

Keep combustible decor away from open flames and follow product and venue instructions. Use a flameless option when the setup cannot be supervised.

Where should a backdrop go?

Choose a stable approved spot away from doors and busy walkways, and secure it according to venue or manufacturer guidance.
