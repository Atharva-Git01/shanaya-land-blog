How to Ask Guests Before Posting Their Photos

Make photo sharing comfortable by asking before posting identifiable guests, explaining where images may appear, and respecting a no-photo request.

Short answer

Before publishing an identifiable photo, ask the people shown and explain where you plan to share it. Offer an easy way to decline, and remove or untag a photo if someone changes their mind.

Set expectations before the event

If you plan to take or share photos, mention it in the invitation or at the gathering. Explain whether photos are for a private album, a public social post, or both. Do not assume that attending means someone agrees to be photographed or posted.

Offer a simple opt-out, such as telling the host privately or using a discreet no-photo indicator. Make sure the photographer knows how to respect it.

Ask clearly before sharing

Show the exact photo when possible and ask whether the person is comfortable with that image being shared on the named account or platform. A general “is it okay if I post this?” is clearer than tagging someone after publication.

For group images, ask each identifiable person or use a version that leaves out anyone who declines. Be especially careful with children and follow their parent or guardian’s preferences.

Respect changes and privacy

If someone asks you not to post, do not argue. Remove the image, untag them, or choose another photo. Check the audience and location settings before posting and avoid including private details in captions.

This is courtesy guidance, not a substitute for local privacy or image rules. Requirements can depend on where the event happens and who is pictured.

Quick checklist

  • Tell guests if photos may be shared publicly
  • Ask about the specific image and destination
  • Offer an easy private opt-out
  • Get parent or guardian guidance for children
  • Respect removal requests and review tags/location

Frequently asked questions

Is it enough to ask after tagging someone?

It is more considerate to ask before posting or tagging. Share the image and explain where it will appear so the person can make an informed choice.

What if someone changes their mind after a photo is posted?

Respect the request promptly. Remove the image or tag, and avoid reposting it elsewhere without permission.
