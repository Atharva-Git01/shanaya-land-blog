The Day-Before Birthday Party Checklist

Use the day before to remove friction: prepare what can be done safely, gather supplies, and confirm the last details.

Quick answer: Clear the room, arrange tables and decor, gather serving essentials, confirm food and helpers, and send guests a short arrival note. Keep perishable items stored safely.

Prepare the room

  • Clear walkways and move furniture into the planned layout.
  • Choose stable positions for the cake and food tables.
  • Check seating, lighting, exits, and access to the restroom.
  • Keep cords, bags, and balloon strings out of the walking route.

A comfortable, easy-to-use room matters more than fitting in another decoration.

Set tables and decorations

Put out tablecloths, stands, labels, and reusable decor. Check the photo background and remove packaging or unrelated items from the frame. Keep candles away from fabric and balloons, and follow venue rules for hanging decor.

Check food and serving tools

Confirm pickup times for cake and prepared food. Put shelf-stable items together. Chill drinks or ingredients that can safely be prepared early, and make a note of what must remain refrigerated until close to serving time.

Gather small essentials

  • Plates, napkins, cups, serving spoons, cake knife, and cake server
  • Candles and a lighter or matches stored safely
  • Phone charger, speaker, and playlist
  • Trash bags and containers for leftovers
  • Water and a small cleanup cloth

Send one final note

Share the start time, address or entrance instructions, transport details if useful, and a contact method for delays. Confirm who is handling each helper task, then leave time to rest.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do first?

Clear and arrange the room before decorating. It is easier to place tables once walking paths are set.

Can I leave food out overnight?

No. Follow safe storage guidance for each dish. Keep perishable items refrigerated and set them out close to serving time.
