How to Order a Custom Birthday Cake

Give the baker the date, guest count, flavor, design reference, wording, dietary needs, pickup time, and transport plan. Confirm the details and price in writing.

Short answer

A clear cake brief prevents last-minute surprises. Send the essentials in one message, ask what the bakery needs to confirm the order, and keep the final design, serving count, pickup time, and payment terms together.

Share the practical details

Include the date, event time, guest count, where the cake will be served, and whether you need delivery or pickup. These details affect the design, sizing, and handling.

Describe the design clearly

Send one or two reference images and say which elements matter most: palette, topper, writing, or general style. Mention what can change if the baker needs to adapt the design.

Confirm the order before paying

Ask about flavor, fillings, servings, allergy procedures, deposit, cancellation policy, and collection instructions. Read back the final details in writing.

Quick checklist

  • Event date and pickup/delivery time
  • Cake size and agreed serving count
  • Flavor and filling
  • Design photo and written message
  • Allergy details and cross-contact questions
  • Total price, deposit, balance, cancellation terms
  • Transport and storage advice

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I order?

Timing varies by bakery and design. Ask as soon as you know the date, especially for custom decoration or a busy weekend.

What should I include in a cake order message?

Date, servings, flavor, design reference, message, dietary needs, pickup/delivery, and any storage or transport questions.

Should I send a reference photo?

Yes, if it helps explain the design. Identify the elements you want to use and let the baker confirm what can be reproduced.
