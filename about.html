ABOUT THE SHANAYA DIARIES

Real moments, useful ideas.

Real moments from Shanaya's Instagram, with ideas to make them yours. The Shanaya Diaries turns posts from @_itzzzshanaya into practical guides you can use for your own celebrations, outfits and everyday moments. Shanaya is a dance enthusiast who shares lifestyle, fashion and celebration moments there.

What the blog does

An Instagram post shows a moment. A guide here explains how to recreate the idea yourself: how to plan the party, put the outfit together or set up the decor. The first collection begins with birthday ideas for an outfit, cake and celebration at home or another venue. Future guides will follow other real moments from Shanaya’s public posts.

How the guides are written

  • Each guide answers one practical question in plain language.
  • Guides link to the Instagram post that inspired them, so you can see the idea in real life.
  • Where a guide relies on outside information, such as food safety or venue rules, it links to the sources used.

Who it's for

Anyone aged 16–40 looking for birthday, outfit, dance or everyday lifestyle ideas that are easy to adapt to their own budget and space.
