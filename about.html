ABOUT SHANAYA LAND

The blog behind the posts.

Shanaya Land is the blog companion to Shanaya's Instagram account, @_itzzzshanaya. Shanaya is a dance enthusiast who shares lifestyle, fashion and celebration moments there.

What the blog does

An Instagram post shows a moment. A guide here explains how to recreate the idea yourself: how to plan the party, put the outfit together or set up the decor. The first series is built around Shanaya's 22nd birthday post, with ideas for celebrating at home or at another venue.

How the guides are written

  • Each guide answers one practical question in plain language.
  • Guides link to the Instagram post that inspired them, so you can see the idea in real life.
  • Where a guide relies on outside information, such as food safety or venue rules, it links to the sources used.

Who it's for

Anyone aged 16–40 looking for birthday, outfit, dance or everyday lifestyle ideas that are easy to adapt to their own budget and space.
